---

M.S. student in Computer Software and Theory at **UCAS** (Chengdu Institute of Computer Applications, CAS). I work on **LLM-based multi-agent systems** and on the engineering that makes them hold up outside a demo.

My current focus is **runtime harnesses for agents** — the idea that an agent's scaffolding, not only its weights, decides how well it reasons, and that this scaffolding can be trained and repaired systematically instead of hand-tuned per model. Around that I also work on **inference acceleration** for LLM reasoning and on the **safety and privacy** risks that surface once these systems are deployed.

### 📄 Selected Publications

<sub>Full list on my [website](https://yuranqiu.github.io) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ausT8TMAAAAJ) · [dblp](https://dblp.org/pid/369/7332)</sub>

<br/>

**[Ecdysis: Efficient and Effective Training of Runtime Harnesses for LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11677)**<br/>
<sub>**Ruiqing Yue**, Yu Cui, Zhuoyu Sun, et al. · arXiv 2026</sub><br/>
Aggregates cross-task failure evidence and attributes failures collaboratively to repair runtime harnesses — up to **1.84×** faster harness training and **+18.56%** reasoning accuracy over instance-level self-evolution.

<br/>

**[Refusal is Not Safety! Benchmarking Latent Safety Risks of LLM-Driven Content Humorization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15977)**<br/>
<sub>Yu Cui, **Ruiqing Yue**, Tingyu Li, et al. · arXiv 2026</sub><br/>
Introduces **HumorSafe** to benchmark latent safety risks in LLM-driven humorization; **HumorPIA** further shows these risks can conceal harmful prompt injections behind seemingly safe humorous refusals.

<br/>

**[Spore: Efficient and Training-Free Privacy Extraction Attack on LLMs via Inference-Time Hybrid Probing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23711)**<br/>
<sub>Yu Cui, **Ruiqing Yue**, Hang Fu, et al. · arXiv 2026</sub><br/>
A training-free privacy extraction attack on LLM agent memory via inference-time hybrid probing, reaching a high success rate at low query cost in both black-box and gray-box settings.
